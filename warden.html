About
WardenLocal-first
Warden is a private, self-hosted camera viewer — one screen for every camera, NVR and FOSS tool you already run, with no account on our servers and no telemetry. It exists because most CCTV apps hold footage hostage behind subscriptions, ads or a vendor's cloud; Warden keeps your cameras and your data yours, wherever you choose to store them.
Built by Hamid Yasir & Co. on the belief that the software running your home should be owned by you, not rented to you — free and open-source by default, interoperable with whatever FOSS stack you prefer, and built for the collective good rather than a single company's bottom line.

Help

Suggested setup

Set up Frigate↗ Self-hosted NVR with AI object detection. Feeds it your RTSP/ONVIF cameras and point Warden at its streams. Set up Nextcloud↗ A FOSS cloud you control. Use it for account backup and continuous footage backup via WebDAV. Connect a camera↗ RTSP/ONVIF cameras and NVRs route through go2rtc so any browser can play them — install it once, then add the camera in Warden. FOSS-ify an old camera↗ Turn a cheap ONVIF/RTSP camera into a proper CCTV feed — go2rtc republishes it as WebRTC/WHEP that Warden can view directly.

Getting started

Adding a camera
Tap the + tile on the dashboard, choose a source type from the dropdown, then fill in only the fields that appear. RTSP/ONVIF/NVR cameras route through your self-hosted go2rtc; WHEP, HLS and MJPEG connect directly.
Viewing a camera
Tap any tile to open the live viewer. Tap the video once to show or hide the on-screen controls. Swipe/scroll or use the device back button to return to the dashboard.
Navigation
Every screen has a back arrow in the top-left, and your device or browser's back button always works the same way inside Warden — it never leaves the app or loses your place.

People & access

Multiple accounts
Use "Log in as someone else" in Settings to add another account on this same device — switch between them anytime without signing out.
Sharing with others
In Settings → Manage access, add a person with their own name and password, pick exactly which capabilities they get (audio/talk, PTZ control, device management), and choose whether they see all cameras or only specific ones you select.
Security
Add TOTP two-factor from any FOSS authenticator app (Aegis, andOTP, FreeOTP) or a device passkey (WebAuthn) for faster unlocking. Both are optional and local to this device.

Cloud & backup

Account backup
Encrypts your account list (name + password hash only, never plain-text) with a passphrase you choose and uploads it to your cloud endpoint, so you can restore your logins on a new device.
Footage backup
Warden stays fully offline until you turn on Cloud backup. Once on, it sends a periodic snapshot per camera to Nextcloud (WebDAV) or any S3/custom endpoint you provide — but only while this tab is open, since it's a client-only app with no server component.
Continuous recording & remote retrieval
Browser tabs can't run while closed, so for footage that keeps recording and backing up when Warden itself is offline, point a FOSS NVR you already control — go2rtc or Frigate — at your cameras and let it push finished clips to remote storage itself (e.g. with rclone to Nextcloud/S3). Warden then just plays those clips back through the Playback screen. This is the standard FOSS workaround since no purely client-side app can record while the device sleeps.

Look & feel

Theme
Choose Light or Dark in Settings → Appearance — your choice is remembered on this device.
Automation & AI
Point Warden at any OpenAI/Ollama-compatible endpoint (Ollama, LocalAI, etc.) to enable the "Ask AI" button in the viewer, and set a per-camera webhook to trigger automations in Frigate, Home Assistant or Node-RED.
Warden is built entirely on open standards — RTSP, ONVIF, WebRTC/WHEP, HLS, MJPEG, WebDAV — with no accounts on our servers, no ads, no paywalled features and no telemetry.
