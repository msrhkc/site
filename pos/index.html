How to use the POS

1
Enter client & cashier

Fill in the client's name and phone number, then enter your cashier ID or name.

2
Add items

Enter the item name, description, vessel/source, quantity, unit (kg/lot/item/unit/custom), and rate. Click Add.

3
Add payment lines

Add one or more payment methods (Cash, QR, Card, NPSB, RTGS, Payorder, Cheque, Prepaid). Enter amounts. QR/Card incur 2% fee.

4
References & reimbursement

Enter ref for each payment line. If overpaid, fill reimbursement method and details.

5
Finalise & print

Confirm, then print/save the receipt and gate pass. "Show prices" is checked by default for the office copy — uncheck it to print a broker copy (items & quantities only).

All data stays on this device. No internet connection required.
