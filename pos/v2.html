🧭 How to use the POS

1
Enter client & cashier

Fill in the client's name and phone number, then enter your cashier ID or name.

2
Add items

Select a zone (Precision, Mid‑tier, Scrap, or Miscellaneous). Enter name, vessel, quantity, unit (kg/lot/item/unit/custom), and rate. Click Add.

3
Add payment lines

Add one or more payment methods (Cash, QR, Card, NPSB, RTGS, Payorder, Cheque, Prepaid). Enter amounts. QR/Card incur 2% fee.

4
References & reimbursement

Enter ref for each payment line. If overpaid, fill reimbursement method and details.

5
Finalise & send

Confirm, print/save (optionally as a price-hidden broker copy), and submit to save the receipt and print a gate pass. Sending to the office via WhatsApp is a separate button after saving. The transaction is automatically appended to the master CSV.

All data stays on this device. No internet required except for WhatsApp.
