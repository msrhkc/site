📘 Help & Instructions

This guide explains each section in plain English. All fields are optional unless marked otherwise.

  • Staff Information: Your name and role — helps identify who prepared the report. Optional.
  • Cash in Hand: All physical cash we have at the end of the day. Count the cash drawer, safe, or office cash. Example: 25,450. Optional.
  • Cash at Bank: Copy the closing balance from our banking app. Each account needs a name, account number (required), and balance. Optional.
  • Stock & Money Owed: Total value of unsold stock from our stock register. Also, money customers owe us — only if we sell on credit. Both optional.
  • Owner's Investment: Money the owner put in and money the owner took out. The net figure is calculated for us. Optional.
  • Money We Owe: All debts — bank loans, hand loans, investor money, supplier advances, etc. Enter the lender, date, amount, and interest rate. Optional.
  • Today's Sales: Total money collected from sales today. One figure, no customer breakdown. Optional.
  • Financial Position: This dashboard shows our financial health automatically — Owner's Equity and Recovery Ratio are calculated for us.
  • Notes: Anything important the Attorney‑in‑Fact should know. Optional.
  • Signature: Type your name to confirm the report. Optional.

Workflow:

Fill the form → Generate Preview → Review → Check all boxes → Approve & Submit → WhatsApp summary → Print PDF → Seal & deliver.

All fields are optional. Only business rules (e.g. taken amount cannot exceed added amount) are enforced.
