🧭 How to use the POS

1
Enter client & cashier

Fill in the client's name and phone number, then enter your cashier ID or name.

2
Add items

Select a zone (Precision, Mid‑tier, or Scrap). Enter name, vessel, quantity, and rate. Click Add.

3
Review & choose payment

Check the provisional receipt. Select a payment method (Cash, QR, Card, NPSB, RTGS, Payorder). Fee (2% for QR/Card) is added automatically.

4
Payment overview & reference

See the grand total and payment method. Click the speaker to hear it. Enter the transaction ID or reference.

5
Finalise & send

Confirm, then print, save, or send via WhatsApp. The transaction is automatically appended to the master CSV. Download it anytime from History.

All data stays on this device. No internet required except for WhatsApp.
