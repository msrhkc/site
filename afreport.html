Step-by-Step Instructions (for the Accountant)
This report must be sent to the Attorney-in-Fact every day before 5 PM.

Steps:
1. Enter your name and designation.
2. Enter the amount of cash in hand (at end of day).
3. Add bank accounts — name, account number (required), and closing balance.
4. Enter the Closing Inventory value (from the stock register or ERP). Add Accounts Receivable only if you sell on credit, otherwise leave it blank.
5. Enter Owner's Capital Introduced and Capital Withdrawn.
6. Enter company liabilities — bank loans, hand loans, external investors, party advances, or others — with lender name, date issued, outstanding amount, and annual interest rate. Daily interest expense is calculated automatically.
7. Enter today's Gross Revenue (sales recovery).
8. Write any necessary remarks.
9. Finally, type your name to sign.
10. Click "Generate Preview" and review.
11. Check all checklist boxes and click "Approve & Submit".
12. Send the WhatsApp summary, then follow the instructions to print the PDF, seal it in an envelope, and deliver it to the Attorney-in-Fact.

📘 Help & Instructions

  • Accountant Info: Your full name and designation.
  • Cash in Hand: Count all physical cash at the end of the day.
  • Cash at Bank: Copy the closing balance from your banking app. Each account needs a name, account number (required), and balance.
  • Current Assets: Closing Inventory is the total valuation from your stock register or ERP — one figure, no breakdown. Accounts Receivable is optional and can be left at 0 if you rarely sell on credit.
  • Owner's Capital: Capital introduced and any amount withdrawn are tracked separately so the full history is preserved. Net Owner Investment is calculated automatically.
  • Liabilities: List all amounts the company owes — bank loans, hand loans, external investors, party advances, or others. Include the lender name, date issued, outstanding amount, and annual interest rate. Daily interest expense is calculated automatically.
  • Operations: Today's Gross Revenue — the total sales recovered today. One figure, no customer breakdown.
  • Financial Position: Owner's Equity (Total Assets − Total Liabilities) and Recovery Ratio (Owner's Equity ÷ Owner's Capital Introduced) are calculated automatically.
  • Remarks: Any notes for the Attorney‑in‑Fact.
  • Signature: Type your name to sign off.

Workflow: Fill form → Generate Preview → Review → Check all boxes → Approve & Submit → WhatsApp summary → Print PDF → Seal & deliver.
