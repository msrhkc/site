📘 Help & Instructions

  • Accountant Info: Your full name and designation.
  • Cash on Hand: Count all physical cash at the end of the day.
  • Bank Accounts: Copy the closing balance from your banking app. Each account needs a name, account number (required), and balance.
  • Owner Investment: Total money the owner has put in, and any amount returned. The outstanding balance is calculated automatically.
  • Money the Company Owes: List everyone the company owes — banks, hand loans, investors, suppliers, or others. Include the interest rate to calculate daily interest automatically.
  • Money Recovered Today: Money received back into the business today.
  • Remarks: Any notes for the Attorney‑in‑Fact.
  • Signature: Type your name to sign off.
