Company Background

Mehreen Ship Recycling is a family-owned partnership between Md. Jahurul Alam (Managing Partner) and Lucky Johar (Equity Partner), with three Partner-Designates—Hamid Yasir, Seju Yasir, and Akhi Johar—representing the next generation of ownership. All executive authority originates from the Managing Partner and Equity Partner and is formally delegated to an Attorney-in-Fact, who serves as the primary decision-making authority within the company. Support roles, such as Unit Monitor positions, are designed to relay and implement ownership instructions within specific operational units but carry no independent executive, managerial, or financial authority.

Unit Monitor

An assistant appointed to relay and carry out the owners' instructions within a functional unit. Unit Monitors hold no independent executive, managerial or financial authority. Every unit ultimately answers to the Attorney-in-Fact via Arefin Mahmood (Executive Assistant to the Attorney-in-Fact).

Sub-Unit Monitor

An assistant role within a sub-unit responsible for relaying coordination and instructions, reporting up through the parent unit's Unit Monitor.

All executive authority remains vested in the Attorney-in-Fact under delegated authority from the Managing Partner and Equity Partner. Every functional unit ultimately reports administratively to the Attorney-in-Fact through the Executive Assistant (Audit, Standardization & Administration).
