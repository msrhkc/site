About

Mehreen Ship Recycling

Mehreen Ship Recycling is a HKC compliant shipyard based in Ghoramara, Chittagong, Bangladesh, operating within one of the world's most established ship recycling regions.

The company specializes in the acquisition of end-of-life vessels, responsible ship recycling, material recovery, and the connection of recovered resources with local and international markets.

MSR approaches ship recycling as a structured resource recovery process. Beyond dismantling vessels, the industry connects maritime assets with workers, suppliers, transporters, traders, downstream industries, and buyers through the continued circulation of valuable materials and equipment.

Our Approach

From Vessel Acquisition to Resource Recovery

Ship recycling requires careful planning, industrial knowledge, skilled workers, and effective connection with material markets. MSR manages the recycling process through several key stages.

Stage 01

Acquisition

The process begins with the evaluation and acquisition of end-of-life vessels. Each vessel is assessed according to its condition, material composition, operational history, and commercial suitability.

Stage 02

Planning

Before recycling activities begin, vessels are reviewed and prepared to support organized operations, responsible handling, and efficient recovery of materials.

Stage 03

Dismantling

Vessels are dismantled through structured processes designed around safety, organization, and responsible recovery practices.

Stage 04

Sorting

Recovered materials are separated according to type, quality, and potential application. Steel, machinery, equipment, non-ferrous materials, and reusable components are directed toward appropriate markets.

Stage 05

Market Integration

Recovered resources are connected with local and international buyers, supporting continued use within industrial supply chains.

HKC Responsible Recycling

Safe and Environmentally Sound Practices

Responsible ship recycling requires planning, documentation, controlled operations, and responsible management of recovered materials alongside robust dismantling capability.

MSR is committed to practices aligned with the principles of the Hong Kong International Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships.

Through improved processes and documentation, the company aims to support safer operations, greater transparency, and responsible resource recovery.
