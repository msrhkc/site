Terms and Conditions

Payment Terms

A non-refundable fee of BDT 500 applies to every bid you submit, whether it's accepted or declined. We verify every payment reference against our bank records before reviewing your bid. If your reference is false, invalid, or cannot be verified, you'll be permanently barred from bidding on this platform.

Bidding Terms

All lots are sold as-is, where-is. It's your responsibility to inspect a lot, or arrange an inspection, before you bid. We make no promises about condition, quantity, or fitness for any purpose beyond what's stated in the listing. Submitting a bid is an offer to buy — not a guaranteed purchase. MSR may accept or decline any bid at its sole discretion.

Post-Submission Process

We usually let you know whether your bid was accepted or declined within 48 hours. If accepted, we'll contact you with next steps for payment and collecting your items. Please provide accurate contact information — we may disqualify bids with false, incomplete, or misleading details.
